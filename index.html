A Curious Soul

Born from a fascination with both the tangible and the digital, I am a creator who finds beauty in the intersection of old-world craftsmanship and modern technology.

Every pixel is placed with intention, every animation tells a story. I believe the web should feel like stepping into a living museum — interactive, warm, and endlessly surprising.

When not designing, you'll find me exploring misty forests, collecting vintage curiosities, or lost in the pages of a well-worn book.
